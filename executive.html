How Executive is set up.

  1. Email us or use the contact page. Tell us roughly how many numbers you need and who will be on the setup call — you, your assistant, or your chief of staff.
  2. We agree on monthly ($249) or annual ($2,400) billing and send a Stripe invoice. Your numbers are provisioned once payment is confirmed.
  3. On the setup call we create each number, set its purpose, and configure its screening rules. You install the app on iPhone or Android and start handing out numbers.

Everything on Executive runs on the same screening engine as Personal and Pro. The difference is capacity, and that we configure and maintain it for you.

Questions

Executive FAQ.

Why isn’t Executive sold in the app?

Because it comes with a person. Every Executive account is set up on a call and configured by us, and billing is arranged with your office rather than a card in the app. Personal and Pro are self-serve if you would rather start right away.

Can my assistant manage it?

Yes. The setup call can be with your assistant or chief of staff, and rule changes can go through them. Contact us if you need more than one person with access to the account.

What if I need more than 15 numbers?

Ask. Additional numbers on Executive are arranged case by case. For several protected people in one household or team, Office is the better fit.

How are allowances counted?

Per billing period — your own subscription month or year, not the calendar month. Minutes count connected calls, incoming or outgoing; screening and voicemail do not count. SMS counts outgoing message parts only. Calls already in progress are never cut off at a limit. Full definitions are on the pricing page.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly Executive renews month to month; annual renews yearly. Cancel before the end of the current period and access continues until it ends. Numbers become eligible for release after a 7-day grace period and cannot be recovered once released.
