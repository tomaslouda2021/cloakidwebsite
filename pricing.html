Pricing

Your real number, unlisted.

Every plan includes real US numbers, call screening, SMS, and voicemail. Monthly plans, no contract. Cancel anytime.

One account. Three steps to get started.

  1. Create your CloakID account with your US mobile number — in the iPhone or Android app, or at app.cloakid.app.
  2. Choose Personal or Pro. On iPhone you subscribe in the app through the App Store. On the web and on Android you subscribe through Stripe checkout at app.cloakid.app.
  3. Your first number is set up right after payment is confirmed. Number limits apply to your active numbers. Usage allowances reset monthly, including on annual Executive subscriptions, and are shown below.

Already subscribed? Manage or cancel from Profile → Manage subscription in the app. Executive and Office are arranged with us directly — read about Executive.

A usage period is one month anchored to your subscription start date, not the calendar month. Usage allowances reset monthly, even when you pay annually. Temporary numbers last 7 days and count toward your total. A minute is any connected call on your numbers, incoming or outgoing, rounded up per call; screening time and voicemail are not counted. SMS counts outgoing message parts only (an MMS counts as 3); incoming texts are never counted. When you reach a limit, calls already in progress are never cut — new incoming calls go to voicemail and new outgoing calls and texts are declined until your next monthly allowance reset. You can always release or delete a number.

Subscription details

CloakID Personal ($14.99/month) and CloakID Pro ($34.99/month) are auto-renewing monthly subscriptions. On iPhone they are purchased through Apple In-App Purchase and billed to your Apple ID; on the web and on Android they are billed securely via Stripe. Prices exclude applicable sales tax where it is added at checkout. CloakID Executive ($249/month or $2,400/year) is arranged directly and invoiced through Stripe.

Your subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel anytime; access continues until the period you paid for ends. Usage allowances reset monthly, including on annual Executive subscriptions, and do not roll over.

By subscribing you agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Questions

Pricing FAQ.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Open the app’s side menu → Profile → Manage subscription. If you bought on iPhone, that opens your Apple subscription settings; if you bought on the web or Android, it opens the Stripe billing portal, where you can cancel future renewals, update your payment method, and view invoices. Access continues through your paid period. Billing help.

What happens to my numbers if I cancel?

Access continues through your paid period. After it ends, your numbers become eligible for release following a 7-day grace period. Do not rely on continued service during that grace period. Once a number is released, it cannot be recovered.

Is the price the same in the App Store and on the web?

Yes. Personal is $14.99/month and Pro is $34.99/month wherever you buy. Buy in the iPhone app to pay through Apple, or at app.cloakid.app to pay by card through Stripe. Android users subscribe through the web checkout.

What happens when I hit a limit?

A call that is already connected is never cut off. Once your minutes are used up, new incoming calls go straight to voicemail and new outgoing calls are declined until your next monthly allowance reset. Once your outgoing SMS parts are used up, new outgoing texts are declined; incoming texts keep arriving. The app tells you when you are close to and at a limit. Need more? Move up to Pro from Manage subscription, or talk to us about Executive.

What counts as a replacement number?

Swapping the number on an existing persona, or putting a new number into a slot you freed by releasing one, counts as a replacement for that monthly usage period. Setting up a number in a slot you have never used does not. You can always release or delete a number, even after you have used your replacements — the slot just stays empty until your next monthly allowance reset.

Can I upgrade from Personal to Pro?

Yes, from Profile → Manage subscription in the app. Your numbers, screening rules, messages, and call history all carry over; the higher limits apply from the moment the upgrade is confirmed.

Do you offer team, family, or enterprise plans?

Office is a custom agreement for households and teams. We agree on members, setup support, access, and billing with you before purchase. It is not a self-serve team dashboard. Talk to us.

What does Executive include?

Up to 15 numbers, higher allowances, a setup call with you or your assistant to organize your numbers and configure the screening rules for each one, and priority support. Executive is not sold in the app — here is how it works.

What payment methods do you accept?

On iPhone, whatever payment method is on your Apple ID. On the web and Android, Stripe checkout shows the cards and payment methods available for your device and location. Executive and Office billing is arranged by agreement.
