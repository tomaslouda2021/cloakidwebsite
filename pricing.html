Subscription details

CloakID Principal ($99/month) and CloakID Executive ($2,400/year, or $249/month) are auto-renewing subscriptions billed securely via Stripe. Prices exclude applicable sales tax, which is added at checkout.

Your subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless canceled before the end of the current period. You can cancel anytime; access continues until the period you paid for ends.

By subscribing you agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Questions

Pricing FAQ.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from your CloakID account — no calls, no questions. Access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens to my numbers if I cancel?

Your phone numbers are released at the end of your billing period. Number releases are permanent — you can't get the same number back.

How do I sign up?

Create your account with your mobile number, then subscribe with the same number — your plan activates automatically. On the Executive plan we'll reach out to schedule your concierge setup call.

Do you offer team, family, or enterprise plans?

Yes — that's the Office plan: the principal, spouse, adult children, and assistant under one invoice, administered by your chief of staff or EA. Executive-team contracts billed to the company are available too. Talk to us.

What payment methods do you accept?

Checkout is handled by Stripe — all major cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Link. Office plans can be invoiced.
