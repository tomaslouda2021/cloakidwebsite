The problem

Being reachable shouldn't mean being reachable by everyone.

An investor introduction, a press inquiry, a contractor, a school form. When they all lead to the same number, every part of your life competes for your attention. Give each one its own line, with its own rules for who gets through.

Your next quote request doesn’t need your everyday number. Researchers submitted forms on 105 health-insurance lead websites, using a different phone number for each. They documented third-party data sharing and repeated marketing calls. Read the independent study.

How it works

Three steps to a quieter phone.

Start with an account and a plan. Then decide who each number is for.

01

Create a persona

Choose an available US area code. Name the number Work, Press, or Family and describe the callers you expect.

02

Hand it out instead

Give the persona's number to anyone who asks. Recipients see that number instead of your personal number.

03

Let it screen

Unknown callers state who they are and why they are calling. Your rules help route the call to you, voicemail, or a block.

Personas

A number for every part of your life.

Give colleagues, journalists, family, and service providers different ways to reach you. Each number has its own screening rules, messages, and call history.

  • 3 active numbers on Personal; 6 on Pro; 15 on Executive
  • Set the purpose and screening rules for each line
  • Use a 7-day number within your plan’s total allowance
Screening

A screening assistant for unknown callers.

CloakID asks unknown callers who they are and why they are calling, then evaluates the response against your rules. Approved callers can skip screening. Screening can make mistakes; you can review outcomes and adjust your rules.

  • Screen unwanted calls before deciding whether to ring
  • Let approved callers bypass the questions
  • Review call outcomes in your activity feed

Real SMS & voicemail

Every persona sends and receives real texts and voicemail, with its own caller ID on outbound calls.

Temporary numbers

Your real number stays private

Calls placed through CloakID show your persona’s caller ID. Send messages in CloakID to use that number instead of your personal line.

Security

Privacy you can understand.

Know what stays private, what is stored, and which providers help deliver your calls.

Protected account data

Account phone numbers and stored voicemail audio use encryption. Screening transcripts can remain in call history. Carrier calls and SMS are not end-to-end encrypted. Read about storage and access.

No data selling

Your subscription pays for CloakID. We do not sell your account or communications data. Telephony, transcription, AI, and hosting providers process data to run the service. See who processes it.

Control your account

Cancel billing and request account deletion from your profile. Account records, media, and provider records have different deletion processes. See what deletion covers.

Questions

The short answers.

Are these real phone numbers?

Yes. CloakID provisions real US phone numbers through Telnyx for calls, SMS/MMS, and voicemail. They are internet-based phone numbers, so some verification services may not accept them.

Does CloakID work with iMessage?

CloakID numbers do not use iMessage. Send and receive SMS/MMS in the CloakID app. You can text iPhone and Android users when their carrier supports delivery; your personal iMessage number stays separate.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Open the app’s side menu → Profile → Manage subscription to cancel future renewals in Stripe. Access continues through your paid period. Billing help includes instructions for legacy Apple subscriptions.

What happens when I delete my account?

Deletion removes your account and associated app records and requests release of your numbers. Stored media and certain billing or abuse-prevention records may remain beyond account deletion. Cancel your subscription first and read the deletion details.
