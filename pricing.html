Subscription details

CloakID Principal ($99/month) and CloakID Executive ($2,400/year, or $249/month) are auto-renewing subscriptions billed securely via Stripe. Prices exclude applicable sales tax, which is added at checkout.

Your subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless canceled before the end of the current period. You can cancel anytime; access continues until the period you paid for ends.

By subscribing you agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Questions

Pricing FAQ.

Can I cancel anytime?

Open the app’s side menu → Profile → Manage subscription. For Stripe subscriptions, this opens the billing portal, where you can cancel future renewals, update your payment method, and view invoices. Access continues through your paid period. Grandfathered Apple subscribers manage renewals through Apple. Billing help.

What happens to my numbers if I cancel?

Access continues through your paid period. After it ends, your numbers become eligible for release following a 7-day grace period. Do not rely on continued service during that grace period. Once a number is released, it cannot be recovered.

How do I sign up?

Follow the three steps above. Create your account before checkout and use the same mobile number for both. On Executive, contact our team to arrange a setup call for you or your assistant. If your plan does not appear after payment, contact support before paying again.

Do you offer team, family, or enterprise plans?

Office is a custom agreement for households and teams. We agree on members, setup support, access, and billing with you before purchase. It is not a self-serve team dashboard. Talk to us.

What does concierge setup include?

A setup call with you or your assistant to organize your numbers and configure the screening rules for each one. All plans use AI screening; Executive adds more numbers, higher usage allowances, concierge onboarding, and priority support.

How are usage allowances counted?

Allowances are shared across your account’s numbers. The app reports call minutes and incoming and outgoing SMS usage for the calendar month. Check your account’s usage and contact support if you need more capacity. Acceptable-use rules apply.

What payment methods do you accept?

Stripe checkout shows the cards and payment methods available for your device and location. Office billing is arranged by agreement.
